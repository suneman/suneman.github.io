Last year I had the absolute honor of becoming [*efor*](https://universitetshistorie.ku.dk/leksikon/e/efor/) at Valkendorfs Kollegium. Valkendorfs is the oldest student residence in the Nordic countries, founded in 1589 and tucked away on Sankt Peders Stræde in the middle of Copenhagen. I was lucky enough to live there when I was a student myself, and the place changed my life (among many other things, it's where I met my wife :)

![The coat of arms of Valkendorfs Kollegium in black and white: a shield with three curved wings around a rose, topped by a helmet with a plume, framed by acanthus leaves and a banner reading 'Collegii Valkend'.](/assets/20261117_valkendorf_on_tv/valkendorf_vaabenskjold.jpg "The coat of arms of Valkendorfs Kollegium. Image: MKølkær, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons.")

If you haven't heard of the place, not to worry. These old university dormitories are kind of a secret world that not too many people know about. But no more! There's now a whole TV show that explains everything: DR's series *Huse med hemmeligheder* ("Houses with secrets") spent an episode there this fall:

> Konger, hekse og banebrydende litteratur

The title literally means "Kings, witches and groundbreaking literature". And it is, in fact, a reasonable three-word summary of the place. The founder, Christoffer Valkendorf, crowned Christian IV and also has witch trials and pirates on his CV.

The [list of alumni](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Valkendorfs_Kollegium) is incredible. It includes Grundtvig, Ingemann, Blicher, Ewald and Herman Bang, the last of whom did some of his reinventing of Danish literature while living there. The episode follows two of the current residents, who are living through a much-needed restoration of the building.

It's 43 minutes, in Danish, and you can watch it on DRTV: [Huse med hemmeligheder – Konger, hekse og banebrydende litteratur](https://www.dr.dk/drtv/se/huse-med-hemmeligheder_-konger-hekse-og-banebrydende-litteratur_623434).
